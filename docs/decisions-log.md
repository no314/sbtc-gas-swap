# Decisions log: self-sufficient sponsor for sBTC to STX

Updated 2026-09-05.

## Decided (Werner)

- Scope C: one contract handles both "buy gas" (small) and general sBTC to STX swaps; amount is the only difference.
- Payments: a (fee rebate) to the sponsor in STX, equal to the chosen tier. b (50 bips of sBTC input) to a fixed receiver in sBTC. c (0 to 100 bips of sBTC input) to an optional integrator principal in sBTC. sBTC preferred as the harder asset.
- Tiers 0.01 / 0.1 / 1 STX. No custom fee. Default tier chosen by the SDK from swap amount and relay-published minimum (see open items).
- Slippage: default 10 percent for gas-size swaps; user-adjustable for larger swaps. Min-out must be at least the tier.
- Testing: no reliable testnet pools; simnet with pool mocks plus mainnet dust-size smoke tests.
- Deployers: mainnet SP2BM6AQSMQ04CX8KDE62QBFVZTDZ2ZX80GZJSBZ4, testnet ST2BM6AQSMQ04CX8KDE62QBFVZTDZ2ZX80G22E500. b receiver starts as the deployer, changeable by the contract owner via a function.
- Deliverable priority: the integrable service (contract, relay, SDK, docs) first; the standalone dapp is for testing and demos. Leather primary integrator, Bitflow secondary.
- Single repo in /Users/werner/Claude/Projects/sponsored-sbtc-stx-swap.
- Relay: three sponsor keys, one per tier, designed so more keys per tier can be added in v2 (documented, not built).
- Sponsor payment via tx-sponsor? (permissionless sponsors) accepted; interception concern dismissed as harmless.
- Preflight simulation on the relay is required.

## Build decisions (2026-09-05)

- Pool selection off chain (SDK), whitelist and min-out enforced on chain. DLMM included; skipped below 100 STX output. Ties by STX reserve.
- Fee recipient mutable by the owner (`set-fee-recipient`, `transfer-ownership`); rate (50 bips) and pool whitelist immutable.
- Unsponsored calls refused (`ERR_NOT_SPONSORED u101`).

- Simnet: real mainnet pool sources loaded from `.cache/requirements` for type checking; behaviour tested through mocks with the same arithmetic because simnet cannot run `is-standard` on mainnet principals, seed the real pools, or set `tx-sponsor?`. The simnet build is generated by principal substitution and hash-verified against the mainnet source.
- Relay: one core, Cloudflare Worker and Node adapters; three per-tier keys; `minTier` published; RBF every 10 minutes after 30 minutes pending, never above the tier. (The `feeFactor` dial and the node estimator were removed on 2026-09-29; see below.)

- Disclaimer under New Jersey law, drafted by the build, counsel review before launch.
- Dapp name "sBTC to Stacks gas"; Xverse offered untested (the relay's post-condition check protects users regardless of wallet).

## Naming, fee policy and publishing (2026-09-12)

- Fee names in any user-facing copy: network fee (the tier, repaid to the sponsor in STX), default provider fee (50 bips of the sBTC input), integrator fee (0 to 100 bips). The letters a, b and c stay out of copy. Contract and SDK field names are unchanged: `rebate`, `service-fee`, `integrator-fee`, `serviceFee`.
- Per-tier opening bid. Low bids the market rate as before. Mid opens at twice the low bid. High opens at 80 percent of its tier, which leaves three RBF bumps inside the tier. All three are floors: a hotter fee market still wins, and no tier bids above its own tier. `MID_FIRST_BID_MULTIPLE`, `HIGH_FIRST_BID_PCT` in `wrangler.toml`.
- Rationale and its limit: above the market rate a higher bid buys no speed on Stacks today. The tier decides who keeps the difference, the miner or the sponsor, and the high tier hands it to the miner rather than showing a user who paid 1 STX a 0.003 STX fee.
- RBF wait per tier: low and mid 30 minutes (low bids market, mid already opens high), high 10 minutes, one sweep interval, so the high tier reaches the user's full bid as soon as the mempool rule allows. Werner's call: the high-tier margin goes to the miner, not the sponsor.
- Repository: `github.com/no314/sbtc-gas-swap`, npm scope `@no314`. No website: docs are read on GitHub, the SDK comes from npm.
- Published to stx.fan at `zero_to/gas/`: the demo app, `disclaimer.html` (the relay's `termsUrl`), and `sponsors.json` (the SDK's default relay directory). `scripts/publish-stx-fan.sh` builds, verifies and stages into a sibling `stx-fan` clone; it commits nothing.
- Local working folder split into `sbtc-gas-swap/` (the repo), `stx-fan/` (the publish clone) and `private/` (reference apps, sync snapshots, Claude outputs), so exactly one folder is ever committed.

## The refusal was the fee estimator (2026-09-29, later)

- With the relay's message on the page, the mobile attempt read `TIER_BELOW_MIN: tier low below the current minimum high`. Not mobile, not the wallet, not a signature. The node's fee estimate for the real payload was low 632, middle 632717, high 712364 uSTX while the mempool held 17 transactions with a contract-call p75 of 1051 STX: a few mispriced transactions poison the middle and high estimates. The relay trusted the middle value, computed `minTier` high, and refused every user. `/v1/info` disagreed because it estimated with an empty payload, which the node rejects, so it fell back to the floor and said `low`.
- Fix: the relay trusts the node's low estimate (`ESTIMATE_PERCENTILE`, default `low`), `/v1/info` estimates with a real representative payload (`REPRESENTATIVE_PAYLOAD_HEX`, 616 bytes) so info and sponsor agree, and it publishes the raw `nodeEstimate` beside the percentile so an operator can see a polluted estimator. The incident is pinned as a relay test with the observed numbers. Rationale recorded in `relay.md`: under-bidding is corrected by the RBF ladder, a refusal by nothing.
- The earlier `INTERNAL` remains unexplained; with the app now showing the relay's message, the next one will not be.

## No fee estimator (2026-09-29, decision)

- Werner's call, superseding the "trust the low estimate" fix above: ignore the node's fee estimation altogether. Each bracket opens at the standard minimum we defined (low `3000`, mid `6000`, high `800000` uSTX) and is raised after half an hour (10 minutes for high) on the bracket the user chose, not on any estimate. Differences within a bracket are fine. The per-tier sponsor addresses already keep a low-fee transaction from standing in front of a high-fee one through nonces on one account.
- Implementation: `computeFee` is `min(max(openingBid[tier], bytes * 1), tier)`; `RelayChain.estimateFee`, `FEE_FACTOR`, `FEE_FLOOR_USTX`, `MID_FIRST_BID_MULTIPLE`, `HIGH_FIRST_BID_PCT`, `ESTIMATE_PERCENTILE`, `REPRESENTATIVE_PAYLOAD_HEX`, `nodeEstimate` and `estimatePercentile` are gone. New variables `OPENING_BID_LOW/MID/HIGH` and `MIN_TIER`. `minTier` is now an operator policy, not a market reading; `TIER_BELOW_MIN` means the operator turned that bracket off. `feeEstimate` in `/v1/info` keeps its name for SDK compatibility and carries the opening bids. The incident test now asserts the estimator is never called.
- Accepted risk (Werner): under network congestion the fixed bids may sit until the ladder catches up. The remedy when it matters is more addresses per fee bracket so each account has at most one pending transaction; use of the bridge is low today, so not built.

## Hiro key, twin transactions, and the page that polled a ghost (2026-09-30)

- Every `INTERNAL` seen on 2026-09-29 was HTTP 429 from Hiro on the relay's first chain read (`get-balance`), then the app's 8-second timeout on the retry ("Fetch is aborted"). The key is credited (a bogus key gets 403) but sits on a 50-requests-per-minute, 20-per-second plan, not the 500 RPM Hiro's docs describe; one sponsor request costs 5 to 11 reads. Key rotated; the plan is what it is.
- Wallet `SP16YF6XPB6RNBVJ6KJF28162XQMPJVAWWJYZD279` swapped 550 sats at the low tier: `0xef288fa72cfe9b5b44c6a4a84b369c3cd6bf75ca62ae53c1a14177b68baef533`, origin nonce 0, sponsor nonce 5, fee 3000, block 9096763, `success`, received 1402781 uSTX. The user's page showed `0xe41c362a9fe9d2efac7ddd751e4d7d7410e5247822e3adef0187f6216dbe5070`, which Hiro never saw, and polled it for over 30 minutes. The relay had signed two transactions for that wallet at sponsor nonce 5; the chain took one, the other vanished, nobody paid for it, and the sweep filed both as done. Likely a second submission in quick succession (double click, retry after the timeout, page reset); the user could not be asked.
- Root cause: the relay's nonce accounting (`max(local.next, chain.possible_next)`) is correct, but the Worker keeps it in Cloudflare KV, which is eventually consistent (up to 60 s). Two requests a second apart both read stale state and Hiro's stale `possible_next`. The origin nonce check fails the same way in that window.
- Decisions (Werner): the app follows the swap by origin address and origin nonce, not by txid; a confirmed transaction at the same origin and nonce becomes the result and the original row is labelled "Replaced by fee" (deliberately, even when the twin won by reaching a miner first); the original row is never removed; only the same sender counts. The relay moves nonces, pending records and a short in-flight memory of origin nonces into one Durable Object per tier (single writer, strongly consistent) and refuses a twin before signing with `ORIGIN_NONCE_IN_FLIGHT`; KV keeps only rate limits.
- Deferred, recorded for later: more than one key per tier. It buys throughput and confirmation independence (one transaction in flight per key), not twin prevention; the in-flight reservation does that with one key or many. Trigger to revisit: `pending` per tier in `/v1/info` staying above 1 for long stretches, or `SPONSOR_BUSY` in `wrangler tail`.
- Learnings: KV is not a counter; Hiro lags the node by seconds after a broadcast; origin plus nonce is the stable identity of a swap, a txid is one attempt; never delete the user's record of what they submitted, add the outcome beside it.

## Twins: built (2026-10-09)

- App: the Done page follows `(origin, nonce)` through three reads per tick (the relay's txid, the wallet's confirmed transactions, its mempool), keeps the original row and marks it "Replaced by fee" when another transaction at that nonce mined, lists mempool twins as "Also pending", and carries `origin` and `nonce` in the URL so a reload resolves without the relay result. The origin nonce is read from the wire bytes the wallet returned (`originNonceFromHex`). Fixtures `tx-replaced`, `tx-other-sender`, `tx-twin-pending`; 185 harness checks.
- Relay: the store gained atomic `allocateNonce` / `releaseNonce` / `settleNonces` and the origin reservation (`reserveOriginNonce`; a running reservation expires after 2 minutes, a broadcast one after 6 hours or when the sweep files the origin nonce as executed). The Worker keeps that state in a Durable Object `TierState` (instances `low`, `mid`, `high`, `origins`; SQLite-backed, free plan); KV keeps rate limits only. The reservation is per origin, not per key, because a twin can arrive on another tier. New verdicts: `ORIGIN_NONCE_IN_FLIGHT` (409, final for the SDK), `TIER_TOO_SMALL` (400, the admission-floor guard), `UPSTREAM_RATE_LIMITED` (503 for a chain 429, previously `INTERNAL`). The chain client retries twice (1 s, 2 s) inside the SDK's 8 s timeout.
- SDK: `admissibleTiers`, `sponsoredByteLength`, `admissionFloor`, for integrators who want to grey out tiers before the wallet opens; the demo app does not use them, since every tier covers a 680-byte swap fourteen times over.
