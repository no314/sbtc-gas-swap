# Decisions log: self-sufficient sponsor for sBTC to STX

Updated 2026-09-05.

## Decided (Werner)

- Scope C: one contract handles both "buy gas" (small) and general sBTC to STX swaps; amount is the only difference.
- Payments: a (fee rebate) to the sponsor in STX, equal to the chosen tier. b (50 bips of sBTC input) to a fixed receiver in sBTC. c (0 to 100 bips of sBTC input) to an optional integrator principal in sBTC. sBTC preferred as the harder asset.
- Tiers 0.01 / 0.1 / 1 STX. No custom fee. Default tier chosen by the SDK from swap amount and relay-published minimum (see open items).
- Slippage: default 10 percent for gas-size swaps; user-adjustable for larger swaps. Min-out must be at least the tier.
- Testing: no reliable testnet pools; simnet with pool mocks plus mainnet dust-size smoke tests.
- Deployers: mainnet SP2BM6AQSMQ04CX8KDE62QBFVZTDZ2ZX80GZJSBZ4, testnet ST2BM6AQSMQ04CX8KDE62QBFVZTDZ2ZX80G22E500. b receiver starts as the deployer, changeable by the contract owner via a function.
- Deliverable priority: the integrable service (contract, relay, SDK, docs) first; the standalone dapp is for testing and demos. Leather primary integrator, Bitflow secondary.
- Single repo in /Users/werner/Claude/Projects/sponsored-sbtc-stx-swap.
- Relay: three sponsor keys, one per tier, designed so more keys per tier can be added in v2 (documented, not built).
- Sponsor payment via tx-sponsor? (permissionless sponsors) accepted; interception concern dismissed as harmless.
- Preflight simulation on the relay is required.

## Build decisions (2026-09-05)

- Pool selection off chain (SDK), whitelist and min-out enforced on chain. DLMM included; skipped below 100 STX output. Ties by STX reserve.
- Fee recipient mutable by the owner (`set-fee-recipient`, `transfer-ownership`); rate (50 bips) and pool whitelist immutable.
- Unsponsored calls refused (`ERR_NOT_SPONSORED u101`).
- DLMM post-condition on sBTC is `lte` (partial fills); XYK and Velar are `eq`.
- Simnet: real mainnet pool sources loaded from `.cache/requirements` for type checking; behaviour tested through mocks with the same arithmetic because simnet cannot run `is-standard` on mainnet principals, seed the real pools, or set `tx-sponsor?`. The simnet build is generated by principal substitution and hash-verified against the mainnet source.
- Relay: one core, Cloudflare Worker and Node adapters; three per-tier keys; `feeFactor` dial; `minTier` published; RBF every 10 minutes after 30 minutes pending, never above the tier.
- Hosting: Cloudflare Worker only for v1; Docker path documented as optional. Measured 6.6 ms per sponsor request in Node against the 10 ms free-plan CPU limit; confirm on Cloudflare.
- Disclaimer under New Jersey law, drafted by the build, counsel review before launch.
- Dapp name "sBTC to Stacks gas"; Xverse offered untested (the relay's post-condition check protects users regardless of wallet).

## Naming, fee policy and publishing (2026-09-12)

- Fee names in any user-facing copy: network fee (the tier, repaid to the sponsor in STX), default provider fee (50 bips of the sBTC input), integrator fee (0 to 100 bips). The letters a, b and c stay out of copy. Contract and SDK field names are unchanged: `rebate`, `service-fee`, `integrator-fee`, `serviceFee`.
- Per-tier opening bid. Low bids the market rate as before. Mid opens at twice the low bid. High opens at 80 percent of its tier, which leaves three RBF bumps inside the tier. All three are floors: a hotter fee market still wins, and no tier bids above its own tier. `MID_FIRST_BID_MULTIPLE`, `HIGH_FIRST_BID_PCT` in `wrangler.toml`.
- Rationale and its limit: above the market rate a higher bid buys no speed on Stacks today. The tier decides who keeps the difference, the miner or the sponsor, and the high tier hands it to the miner rather than showing a user who paid 1 STX a 0.003 STX fee.
- RBF threshold cut from 30 minutes to 10 minutes, one sweep interval, so a stuck transaction is bumped at the first sweep.
- Repository: `github.com/no314/sbtc-gas-swap`, npm scope `@no314`. No website: docs are read on GitHub, the SDK comes from npm.
- Published to stx.fan at `zero_to/sbtc-gas/`: the demo app, `disclaimer.html` (the relay's `termsUrl`), and `sponsors.json` (the SDK's default relay directory). `scripts/publish-stx-fan.sh` builds, verifies and stages into a sibling `stx-fan` clone; it commits nothing.
- Local working folder split into `sbtc-gas-swap/` (the repo), `stx-fan/` (the publish clone) and `private/` (reference apps, sync snapshots, Claude outputs), so exactly one folder is ever committed.
